Privacy Policy

Who operates Speneo

Speneo is operated by Rahul, an independent developer. For privacy questions or requests about your personal data, email official.speneo@gmail.com.

Account and profile

Speneo uses Firebase Authentication for Google and email/password sign-in. Firebase handles password verification and password reset emails. Speneo processes your account identifier, name, email address and profile photo information to create and identify your account. Profile details you provide, such as birth date and gender, are used to maintain your profile.

Financial records and preferences

Speneo processes the information you enter, including your country or currency preference, salary and payday, transactions and their notes and categories, recurring expenses and savings goals. It uses these details to show your records, calculate totals and support monthly planning.

Device storage and sync

Speneo stores records and settings on your device so you can use supported features offline. It sends account and financial records to its backend to save changes and sync them across signed-in devices. The release app uses HTTPS for this connection. The backend uses MongoDB to store synced records.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Speneo uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver them. Firebase may process an app installation or messaging identifier for delivery. You can change notification permission in Android settings.

Editing and deleting records

You can edit supported profile fields in Account Settings and delete individual transactions in the app. Deleting a transaction removes it from your active records, and the deletion syncs when the app connects. Signing out or uninstalling Speneo does not delete the cloud account or its saved records.

Account deletion and retention

Email official.speneo@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in and ask us to delete your Speneo account and its data. We may ask you to verify ownership. Please keep passwords and financial details out of your message. Once your deletion request is verified, Speneo retains your account data for up to seven days before permanently deleting it, except where retention is required by law.
